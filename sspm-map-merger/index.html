        How it works

        • Private. Everything runs in your browser. Your maps are never uploaded.
        • Audio from each map is decoded, joined end to end (with your chosen gap) and re-encoded as one MP3 so the whole pack is a single song.
        • Notes from each map are moved to the point where its song starts in the merged audio, then everything is sorted by time.
        • Difficulty of the pack is the hardest map in it. Mappers from all maps are credited. Only note markers are carried over.
        • Cover comes from the map you choose (default: the first).
        • Limits: SSPM v2 files only, 50 maps max. Browsers that can't decode OGG (some Safari versions) can't merge OGG-audio maps — use Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

        MP3 encoding by lamejs (LAME, LGPL). Not affiliated with Sound Space Plus or Rhythia.
